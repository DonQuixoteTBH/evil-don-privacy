Privacy Policy

Evil Don's Department of Bad Decisions

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Evil Don's Department of Bad Decisions respects your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Offline Use

The app can be used in offline mode without transmitting scenario-generation information to an external AI service.

Online AI Features

If you choose to use online AI features, information necessary to generate scenarios is transmitted to the AI provider you select. This may include scenario settings such as complexity and flavor, scenario history, rejected concepts, and inventory or scenario summaries.

Depending on the provider selected by the user, this information may be transmitted to OpenAI or Google's Gemini service. These services process the information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

API Keys

Online AI functionality may require an API key supplied by the user. The key is used to authenticate requests with the selected AI provider.

Information We Do Not Collect

The app does not require a user account and does not intentionally collect personal information such as your name, email address, physical address, contacts, financial information, health information, precise location, photos, videos, or audio recordings.

Advertising and Analytics

The app does not contain advertising or third-party advertising SDKs. The app does not use third-party analytics services to track users.

Data Choices

Transmission of scenario-generation information to an external AI provider occurs only when online AI functionality is used. Users may instead use the app's offline functionality.

Changes to This Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy may be directed to the developer using the contact information provided on the app's Google Play listing.
